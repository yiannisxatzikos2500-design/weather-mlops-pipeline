Under Athens' skies, so bright and wide,
Άθανας ο καιρός, δεν υπάρχει βροχή,
The temperature rises, a gentle, warm tide,
19 degrees, a perfect day to reside.

In Rome, the sun shines with a warmer grin,
Ρώμη, πόλη του ήλιου, ζεστό και καθαρό,
22 degrees, a delight to step within,
Ανέβαινει η ζέστη, με γλυκό χαμόγελο.

Aalborg's climate, cooler, yet serene,
Άαλμποργκ, πόλη της DEN, ψύχραιμη και ήρεμη,
18 degrees, a gentle breeze, a peaceful scene,
Δεν υπάρχει βροχή, μόνο ήλιος και χαρά.

Note: Please keep in mind that the poem is a creative expression and not a direct translation. The Greek part is written in a way that rhymes and flows well, while trying to maintain the same meaning and atmosphere as the English part.
