In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα ζεσταίνει (Athina zestainei),
Thirty degrees, a summer's sway,
No rain falls, ούτε μια σταγόνα (oute mia stagona),
A dry and sunny day.

In Rome, η Ρώμη (Romi) burns with might,
Thirty-three degrees, a scorching light,
A drop of rain, μια μικρή σταγόνα (mia mikri stagona),
A rare and brief delight.

But Aalborg, η Άλμποργκ (Alborg) differs far,
Nineteen degrees, a cool and rainy star,
Three millimeters, τρία χιλιοστά (tria chiliorsta) fall,
A soothing balm for the city's wall.
