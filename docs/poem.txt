Στη Αθήνα, η θερμοκρασία υψώνεται,
In Athens, the temperature rises high,
31 degrees, a sweltering summer's sigh.
Στην Ρώμη, λίγο χαμηλότερα,
In Rome, a little lower it stands,
29 degrees, with sunny hands.

Στην Άλμποργκ, η ψύχρα αέρας παραμένει,
In Aalborg, the cool air remains,
20 degrees, with gentle, soothing rains.
Αλλά σήμερα, όχι μία σταγόνα,
But today, not a single drop,
Ο καιρός είναι καθαρός, без một κρότου.

(Note: Please keep in mind that the poem is written in a way that the Greek and English lines alternate, trying to maintain a coherent meaning and rhythm. However, it's not a direct translation, but rather an adaptation to fit the poetic style.)
