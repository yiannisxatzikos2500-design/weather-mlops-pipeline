In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα λάμπει (Athens shines),
26 degrees, a warm delight.
In Rome, it's hotter, η Ρώμη καίγεται (Rome burns),
30 degrees, a summer's night.

But far north, in Aalborg's Chill, στο Άαλμποργκ του Βορρά (in Aalborg of the North),
16 degrees, the rain does fall, η βροχή πέρα (the rain beyond).
5.8mm, a drop in the sea, στα 5.8 χιλιοστά, μια σταγόνα στη θάλασσα (at 5.8 millimeters, a drop in the sea),
Aalborg's weather, a different tale, ο καιρός του Άαλμποργκ, μια διαφορετική ιστορία (the weather of Aalborg, a different story).

Note: The temperatures and rain amounts are incorporated into the poem to maintain the bilingual aspect and to compare the weather in the three cities.
