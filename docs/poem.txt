Στ' Αθína (In Athens),
where sunshine warms the day,
23 degrees, a gentle breeze,
όχι βροχή (no rain) comes to stay.

Ρώμη (Rome) nearby, somewhat cooler lies,
20 degrees, with raindrops in the skies,
2mm fall, a light refrain,
βροχή σπάνια (rare rain) brings a soothing strain.

In Αaalborg's north, a different tale,
16 degrees, with skies so pale,
no rain in sight, a dry, cool air,
ένα ξηρό (a dry) atmosphere, beyond compare.
