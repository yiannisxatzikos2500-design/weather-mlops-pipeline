In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα σιγόβραze (Athens sighs)
34 degrees, a sweltering gaze.
Not a drop, ουδέ μια σταγόνα (not a drop)
Falls from the sky, in this dryazona.

In Rome, the sun beats strong, ο ήλιος χτυπά (the sun beats)
37 degrees, all day long.
No rain, keine regen, όχι βροχή (no rain)
To cool the streets, in this eternal summer's song.

In Aalborg, a milder breeze, ένας ήπιος άνεμος (a gentle breeze)
26 degrees, a pleasant tease.
No rain, ingen regn, όχι βροχή (no rain)
To disturb, the Danish peace that it releases.
