In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα ζεσταίνεται (Athens warms up),
The sun beats down with fiery might.
In Rome, it's hotter, Ρώμη καίει (Rome burns),
35 degrees, a sweltering sight.

But far north, in Aalborg's gentle breeze,
Στο Ααλμποργκ, η δροσιά (In Aalborg, the coolness),
22 degrees, a pleasant ease.
No rain falls, ουρανός καθαρός (clear sky),
In all three cities, a sunny day.
