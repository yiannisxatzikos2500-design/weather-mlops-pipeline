In Athens' warmth, η άνοιξη ξεθάβει (spring awakens),
25 degrees of sunshine on my skin,
No rain to spoil the η Αθήνα της χαράς (Athens of joy),
A perfect day to explore and grin.

In Rome, η Ρώμη της ομορφιάς (Rome of beauty) shines,
With 20 degrees and rain that gently falls,
17.8mm of water from above,
A refreshing touch to the Eternal City's walls.

Aalborg, Denmark's hidden gem,
 Lies under grey and colder skies,
12.75 degrees and 3.4mm of rain,
A chilly η μέρα της Βόρειας Ευρώπης (day of Northern Europe),
A time for hot coffee and introspective sighs.

Note: The Greek phrases used in the poem are:
- η άνοιξη ξεθάβει (i anixi xeθavi) meaning "spring awakens"
- η Αθήνα της χαράς (i Athina tis charas) meaning "Athens of joy"
- η Ρώμη της ομορφιάς (i Romi tis omorfias) meaning "Rome of beauty"
- η μέρα της Βόρειας Ευρώπης (i mera tis Vorias Evropis) meaning "day of Northern Europe"
