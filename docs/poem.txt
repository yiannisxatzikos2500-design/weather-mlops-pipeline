Heat waves dance upon my skin,
 Ở Αθήνας (In Athens), where sunbeams grin,
32 degrees, a burning fire,
Και η Βροχή (And the rain), zero, my heart's desire.

Ρώμη (Rome), a close second place,
With 30 degrees, a warm, sunny face,
Ούτε μια σταγόνα (Not a single drop), of rain to sight,
A dry, Italian summer's delight.

But far north, in Άαλμποργκ (Aalborg)'s shade,
A cooler breeze, with rain, is displayed,
19 degrees, a gentle, soothing treat,
And 9.5mm, of rain, to calm the heat.
