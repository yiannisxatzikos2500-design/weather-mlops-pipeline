In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα ζεστάνει (Athens heats up),
Temperature soars, 29 degrees high θερμοκρασία φθάνει στα 29.
Rome's sun shines bright, Ρώμη λαμπρύνει (Rome shines),
32 degrees, a scorching sight, η πόλη καίγεται (the city burns).

But far north, Ααλμποργκ διαφέρει (Aalborg differs),
Aalborg's cooler breeze, δροσιάζει τον αέρα (cools the air),
20 degrees, a gentle hush, η ησυχία της πόλης (the city's quiet),
A respite from the heat's flush, αναπαύση από τη ζέστη (rest from the heat).

Note: Please keep in mind that the translation is poetic and may not be a direct word-for-word translation, but rather an attempt to convey the same meaning and feeling in both languages.
