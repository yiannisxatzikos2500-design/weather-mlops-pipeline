Σήμερον, τρεις πόλεις, τρεις καιροί (Today, three cities, three weathers)
In Athens, warm sunshine does abide,
Η Αθήνα, ζεστή και εύθυμη (Athens, warm and cheerful)
21 degrees, a gentle, sunny pride.

 Tại Ρώμη, (In Rome) the temperature's high,
23 degrees, a beautiful, clear sky,
Ο ουρανός καθαρός, (The sky is clear)
No rain to spoil the day's delight.

But far away, in Aalborg's chilly grasp,
9 degrees, a different story to clasp,
Στην Ααλμποργκ, (In Aalborg) the cold does bite,
No rain, but winter's chill, a contrasting sight.
