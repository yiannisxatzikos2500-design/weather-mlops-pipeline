In Athens' gentle breeze, η Αθήνα σφυρίζει,
16 degrees warmth does quietly cease.
Rome's sun shines bright, Ρώμη λάμπει,
20 degrees of heat, in a lovely ease.

Aalborg's chill, ο Άαλμποργκ παγώνει,
9 degrees of cold, with a gentle freeze.
Rain falls softly, Βροχή πέφτει,
Athens 4, Aalborg 3, Rome's skies appease.

(Note: The poem is written in a way that the English and Greek lines alternate, with the Greek lines providing a translation of the English lines. However, please note that poetry translation can be subjective, and the Greek lines may not be a word-for-word translation.)
