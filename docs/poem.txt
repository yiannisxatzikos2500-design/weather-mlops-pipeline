In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα σιγår (Athens sighs)
With temperatures high, 35 degrees in the skies
Not a drop of rain, όχι μία σταγόνα (no single drop)
A scorched summer day, καύσωνας (heatwave) in every way

Rome's not far behind, η Ρώμη κοντά (Rome close by)
36 degrees of heat, που καίει η γη (burning earth)
No rain in sight, không βρέχει (it doesn't rain)
A dry and sweltering night, νύχτα ζεστή (warm night)

But Aalborg's different, η Ålborg διαφέρει (Aalborg differs)
24 degrees and rain, βροχή ψιχαλιστή (light rain)
A cooler respite, ψύχη αναπνοή (cool breath)
From the heat of Athens and Rome's burning breath, η ψύχη του βορρά (the breath of the north)
