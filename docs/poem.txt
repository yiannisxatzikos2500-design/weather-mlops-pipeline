In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα καίγεται (Athens burns)
36 degrees, a scorching fate,
Not a drop of rain, keine Tropfen (no drops)
To soothe the soul, η ψυχή panting late.

In Rome, the temperature slightly dips,
35 degrees, η Ρώμη (Rome) still sizzles and trips,
No rain in sight, όχι βροχή (no rain)
The Eternal City, η αιώνια πόλη, in heat's chains.

But Aalborg, what a different tale,
21 degrees, a gentle breeze that prevails,
1mm of rain, μια σταγόνα (a drop)
Brings life to the city, η πόλη revives and sails.
