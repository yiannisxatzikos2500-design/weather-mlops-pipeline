In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα καίγεται (Athens burns)
with temperatures so high, 36 degrees in the sky.
Not a drop of rain, όχι μια σταγόνα βροχής (not a single drop of rain)
to relief the heat, only sunshine remains.

Rome's temperature, η Ρώμη θερμά (Rome warm)
follows close, at 34 degrees, a similar form.
No rain in sight, καμία βροχόπτωση (no rainfall)
just sunbeams bright, in the Italian light.

But far north, in Aalborg's chill, στο ψύχος του Άαλμποργκ (in Aalborg's chill)
15 degrees, a different tale to tell, μια διαφορετική ιστορία (a different story).
Rain falls gently, η βρόχη πέφτει με нежάδα (the rain falls gently)
0.8mm, a soothing sound, ένα συναρπαστικό ήχο (an enchanting sound).
