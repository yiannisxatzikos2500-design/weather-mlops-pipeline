In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα σIZζει (Athens burns),
27 degrees, a sunny haze.
No rain falls, ο ουρανός είν' ανοιχτός (the sky is open),
A perfect day, without a care or cost.

In Rome, il caldo è ancora più forte (the heat is even stronger),
31 degrees, a scorching, endless resort.
No clouds gather, ο ήλιος λάμπει (the sun shines),
A city alive, with passion that never sleeps.

But in Aalborg, η θερμοκρασία είναι πιο χαμηλή (the temperature is lower),
18 degrees, with rain that starts to seep.
6.4mm falls, η βροχή είναι εδώ (the rain is here),
A cooler day, with a different atmosphere to keep.

(Note: Please keep in mind that the Greek translations are in a more poetic and literal sense, and may not be exactly grammatically correct or idiomatic. The goal is to convey the essence and feeling of the poem, rather than a precise translation.)
