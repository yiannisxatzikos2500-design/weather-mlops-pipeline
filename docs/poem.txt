In Athens' warmth, η Αθήνα λάμπει (Athens shines),
Where temperatures rise, high and bright, 24 degrees in sight.
A gentle sprinkle, just 2mm of rain,
Βρέχει ελαφρά, (rains lightly), a pleasant summer refrain.

In Rome, the sun, ο ήλιος, shines with less might,
18 degrees, a cooler, more gentle night.
The rain falls soft, 3.6mm from above,
Βρέχει ήπια, (rains softly), a calming, endless love.

But in Aalborg, the chill begins, ο ψύχος αρχίζει,
11 degrees, and rain that pours, 5.6mm within.
Βρέχει πολύ, (rains heavily), the clouds are gray,
Aalborg's weather, a different tale to say.

(Note: The Greek text uses the polytonic system, but I've used the monotonic system which is more commonly used in modern Greek. Also, please note that the temperatures and rain amounts are used as provided, but in a poetic context, they might not be exact.)
