In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα ζεστή (Athina zestee),
The sun beats down, with temperatures high and bright.
34 degrees, a sweltering sight, η θερμοκρασία ανεβαίνει (ee thermokrasia anevainei),
Not a drop of rain, just sun-kissed light.

In Rome, the temperature rises high, Ρώμη καύσωνας (Roomee kafsonas),
35 degrees, the heat does not subside, η ζέστη παραμένει (ee zestee paramenei),
A city of passion, where love does reside, στην πόλη της αγάπης (stee polee tis agapees),
Under the sun, with not a cloud to hide.

But in Aalborg, the temperature's gentle breeze, στο Άαλμποργκ δροσιά (stoo Aalborgk drosia),
24 degrees, a pleasant summer's ease, ένα ήπιο καλοκαίρι (ena eepio kalokairi),
A city of contrasts, where rain may soon appease, μια πόλη αντίθεσεων (mia polee antetheeson),
But for now, the sun shines bright, with not a drop to freeze.
