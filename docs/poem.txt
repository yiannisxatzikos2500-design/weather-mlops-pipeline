Here's a short bilingual poem in English and Greek:

In Athens, η Αθήνα blooms with heat,
32 degrees, not a cloud to meet.
No rain falls, ο ουρανός is bright,
A perfect day, without a fight.

In Rome, η Ρώμη sizzles with might,
37 degrees, a scorching light.
No rain in sight, η ξηρασία reigns,
A sunny day, with no cooling rains.

But Aalborg, η Άαλμποργκ, tells a tale,
31 degrees, a slightly cooler gale.
No rain falls, but the air is fresh,
A pleasant day, with a gentle breath.

 Translation notes:
- η Αθήνα (i Athina) means "the Athens"
- ο ουρανός (o ouranos) means "the sky"
- η Ρώμη (i Romi) means "the Rome"
- η ξηρασία (i xerasia) means "the drought"
- η Άαλμποργκ (i Alborg) is the Greek pronunciation of Aalborg

Please note that the temperatures are unusually high and might not reflect the average weather conditions in these cities.
