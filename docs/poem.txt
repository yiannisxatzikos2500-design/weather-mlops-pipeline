In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα ζεσταίνει (Athens warms),
31 degrees, a sunny sky to greet,
Just 1mm of rain, to slightly cool the beat.

In Rome, la Roma, η Ρώμη, shines so bright,
30 degrees, with not a drop in sight,
A dry and warm day, to explore and delight.

But Aalborg, Άαλμποργκ, tells a different tale,
17 degrees, with raindrops that prevail,
10mm of rain, to soothe the city's gale.

Translation note: The poem uses the Greek alphabet for the city names, "η Αθήνα" for Athens, "η Ρώμη" for Rome, and "Άαλμποργκ" for Aalborg, which is a phonetic transcription of the Danish city name into Greek.
