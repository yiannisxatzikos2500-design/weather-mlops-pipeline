In Athens' warmth, η Αθήνα λάμπει (Athens shines)
With 22 degrees, a gentle breeze.
No rain falls, όχι βροχή (no rain)
Just sunshine, ηλιόλουστο (sunny) days.

In Rome, η Ρώμη (Rome) boasts
A higher temperature, 26 degrees the most.
Not a drop, keine Tropfen (no drops)
Just clear skies, καθαρός ουρανός (clear sky).

But in Aalborg, ο Ααλμποργκ (Aalborg) is cool
15 degrees, a different rule.
No rain, keine Regen (no rain)
Just a calm, γαλήνιος (calm) atmosphere reigns.
