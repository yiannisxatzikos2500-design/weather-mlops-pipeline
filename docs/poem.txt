Here's a short bilingual poem:

Αθήνα, Ρώμη, και Άλμποργκ, πόλεις τρεις,
Athens, Rome, and Aalborg, three cities fair,
Μαζι συγκρίνονται, το καιρό να δουν,
Compared together, to see the weather share.

Η Αθήνα καίει, 32 度 Celsius το θερμόμετρο,
Athens burns, 32 degrees Celsius the thermometer shows,
Η Ρώμη καίει, 34 度 Celsius, χωρίς βροχή,
Rome burns, 34 degrees Celsius, without a drop of rain's woes.

Άλμποργκ, δροσιά, 20 度 Celsius, με λίγη βροχή,
Aalborg, cool, 20 degrees Celsius, with a little rain's sigh,
Διαφορά μεγάλης, στις τρεις πόλεις, το καιρό να δουν,
A big difference, in the three cities, to see the weather's tune.

(Note: The temperatures are rounded to the nearest whole number for simplicity)

Translation:
Athens, Rome, and Aalborg, three cities,
Compared together, to see the weather.
Athens burns, the thermometer shows,
Rome burns, without a drop of rain.
Aalborg, cool, with a little rain,
A big difference, in the three cities, to see the weather.
