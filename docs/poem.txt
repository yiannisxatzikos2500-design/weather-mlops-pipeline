In Athens' heat, η άσφαλτος λάμπει (the asphalt shines),
30 degrees Celsius, a summer's delight.
No rain in sight, καθαρός ο ουρανός (the sky is clear),
A perfect day, in this ancient light.

In Rome, the temperature's slightly lower, 
26 degrees, a warm but gentle breeze.
Η Ρώμη σαγηνεύει (Rome enchants), with its history and charm,
No rain to spoil, this Italian dream.

But in Aalborg, the weather's quite different,
20 degrees, a cooler, more Nordic climate.
Ο καιρός είναι δροσερός (the weather is cool), 
A refreshing change, from the Mediterranean heat's might.
