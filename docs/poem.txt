In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα ζεσταίνει (Athens warms)
With 33 degrees, a sunny day's breeze,
No rain in sight, just clear blue skies,
Ο ουρανός είναι γαλανός (the sky is blue).

In Rome, the temperature rises high,
 Η Ρώμη καίγεται (Rome burns) with 36 degrees in the sky,
Not a drop of rain, just sun's warm light,
Η ζέστη είναι έντονη (the heat is intense) day and night.

Aalborg, cooler, with 27 degrees so fair,
Η Άαλμποργκ είναι δροσερή (Aalborg is cool) with a gentle air,
No rain to spoil, just a pleasant day,
Μια ημέρα ηλιόλουστη (a sunny day) in its own way.
