In Athens' gentle breeze I stand,
Αθήνα, city of golden sand.
The temperature, a pleasant 18 degrees,
Ο καιρός, ηλιόλουστος, μεθυστικός.
Rome's warmth shines brighter still,
Ρώμη, πόλη του φωτός, του έρωτα.
21 degrees, a sunny day,
Όχι μια σταγόνα, chỉ blue skies at play.
But Aalborg's chill begins to bite,
Ααλμποργκ, πόλη του βορρά, ψυχρή νύχτα.
6 degrees, with rain's sad sigh,
Βροχή, 3.2 χιλιοστά, ο ουρανός κλαίει.

(Note: The poem is written in a way that the English and Greek lines are not direct translations of each other, but rather complementary and comparative. This allows for a more poetic and creative expression.)
