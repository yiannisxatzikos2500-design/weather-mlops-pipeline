Στη Αθήνα, η θέα είναι καλή, 
In Athens, the view is fine,
Θερμοκρασία 29, ήλιος λαμπρός.
Temperature 29, sunshine all divine.

Στη Ρώμη, η ζέστη είναι πύρ,
In Rome, the heat is a burning fire,
30 độ, κανείς δεν παραπονιέται.
30 degrees, no one complains or tires.

Στο ΄Ααλμποργκ, η Βροχή πέφτει,
In Aalborg, the rain falls slow,
18 độ, ο καιρός είναι ψύχος.
18 degrees, the weather's chill does blow.

(Translation note: The poem follows the general structure and rhythm of traditional Greek poetry, with a loose translation into English. The temperature and rain values are incorporated into the poem to reflect the given data.)
