In Athens' heat, η Άθηνα ζεσταίνεται (Athina zestainetai),
The sun beats down with fiery might,
32 degrees, a sweltering sway,
As scarce rain falls, just 0.8mm in sight.

In Rome, η Ρώμη (Ro̱mi̱) basks in golden light,
With temperatures at 30 degrees so bright,
A gentle shower falls, just 0.3mm to share,
A soothing respite from the heat that's hard to bear.

But in Aalborg, Αάλμποργκ (Aálborgk) tells a different tale,
With 17 degrees, a mild and gentle gale,
No rain falls, just clear and sunny skies,
Αλλά η ψύχρα αύρα (Allá i psýchra aúra), a cool breeze that sighs.
