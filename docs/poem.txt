Ήλιος καίει στην Αθήνα, 
Sun shines bright in Athens too,
29 degrees of heat upon my skin,
Ζέστη που με παρασύρει.

In Rome, the temperature rises high,
33 degrees, the sun touches the sky,
Δεν βρέχει ούτε σταρ, 
Not a drop of rain, just heat and dry.

But Aalborg's climate tells a different tale,
22 degrees, a milder, cooler gale,
Βροχή μηδέν, 
No rain to speak, just sunshine's sail.

Ήλιος, ζέστη, και διαφοροποίηση,
Sun, heat, and difference, a triplet so fine,
Τρία μέρη, τρεις καιρικές συνθήκες,
Three places, three climates, each unique in design.
