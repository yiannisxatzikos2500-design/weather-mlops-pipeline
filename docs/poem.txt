In Athens' warmth, the sun does shine,
Стεν άστυ, η ζέστη καίει (Sten asty, i zesti kaei)
Temperature soars, a dry delight,
24 degrees, no rain in sight.

Rome's temperatures, a bit more low,
 Η Ρώμη, με ζέστη ήπια (I Rōmī, me zesti īpia)
22 degrees, with rain's gentle touch,
6mm falls, a soothing clutch.

Aalborg's chill, a different tale,
 Η Άλμποργκ, με κρύο σκληρό (I Álmporgk, me kryō skliro)
15 degrees, no rain to claim,
A cool and dry, Nordic plain.
