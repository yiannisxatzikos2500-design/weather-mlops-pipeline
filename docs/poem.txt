In Athens' heat, η Αθήνα ζεστάνει (Athens warms),
27 degrees, a sunny day's delight.
Σαν το Ρώμη, Rome, with temperatures so fair,
25 degrees, without a single raindrop to spare.

But Aalborg's chill, ο ψύχρος του Άαλμποργκ,
18 degrees, a different story to tell.
Αν και ο καιρός είναι καλός, the weather is fine,
No rain falls, in any of these cities divine.

Note: Please keep in mind that the temperatures provided are precise to a large number of decimal places, which is not typical in everyday conversation. The poem uses these temperatures as a basis for comparison, but in a more poetic and less precise way.
