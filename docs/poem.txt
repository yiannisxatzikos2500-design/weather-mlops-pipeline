In Athens' gentle heat, 
ατμόσφαιρα θερμή και γλυκιά (atmosphera thermi kai glykia)
the sun shines bright, 22 degrees so fair,
without a cloud, just 0.4mm of rain to share.

In Rome, the temperature's slightly low,
η Ρώμη με θερμοκρασία χαμηλότερη (i Roma me thermokrasia chameleterei)
21 degrees, with rain that starts to show,
2.7mm, a gentle touch, the skies bestow.

But in Aalborg, the north wind does blow,
ο καιρός στο Άαλμποργκ, πιο κρύος (o kairos sto Aalborg, pio krios)
18 degrees, with rain that falls like tears,
0.9mm, a melancholy that appears.
