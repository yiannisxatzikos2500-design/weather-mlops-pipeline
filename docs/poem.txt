In Athens' warmth, η ζωή κυλά (life flows free),
19.9 degrees, a pleasant spree.
No rain falls, ο ήλιος λάμπει (the sun shines bright),
A beautiful day, in the Greek light.

In Rome, η Ρώμη λάμπει (Rome shines),
20.6 degrees, a warm delight.
A sprinkle of rain, 2.4mm falls,
But the sun still shines, through the Roman walls.

In Aalborg, η πόλη είναι κρύα (the city is cold),
11 degrees, a chill to behold.
A gentle rain, 0.9mm falls,
A grey day, in the Danish calls.

Note: The temperatures and rain measurements are included in the poem to maintain the original data, while the poem itself is a creative interpretation of the weather in each city.
